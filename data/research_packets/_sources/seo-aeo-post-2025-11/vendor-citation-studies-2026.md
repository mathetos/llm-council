## Extractable claims

1. Ahrefs 75K-brand study (Dec 2025 expansion across ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, AI Overviews): branded web mentions correlate r≈0.66 with AI visibility; Domain Rating r≈0.27–0.33; backlinks r≈0.22. Off-site brand signals ~3x stronger than links. (medium)
2. Only ~38% of AI Overview citations come from Google top-10 organic results (down from ~76% mid-2025); pages ranked 11–100 and beyond earn large citation shares, so low/mid-DR pages are not excluded by rank alone. (medium)
3. Ahrefs 55.8M AI Overview study: commercial-intent pages (category, product, lead-gen landing pages) are almost absent from AI Overview citations; how-to and definitional formats dominate. (medium)
4. Same study: structured pages (H2 sections, lists, tables) cited ~2.3x more than unstructured; DR 20–40 pages cited about as often as DR 60–80 when structure and E-E-A-T are present. (medium)
5. Ahrefs 16.975M-citation freshness dataset: AI-cited content averages ~2.9 years old vs ~3.9 years for organic top-10 — a ~26% freshness advantage, not the inflated "4.3x" claim circulating. (medium)

## Notes

Supports the authority-lag assumption: mid/low-DR domains can win citations, but visibility on commercial head terms concentrates in brands with broad earned mentions, and money pages themselves are rarely cited. Route commercial head-term bets through supporting informational assets plus brand-mention building.
