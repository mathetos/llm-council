# Search Console: Search generative AI control

- URL: https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024?hl=en
- Related announcement: https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/new-controls-website-owners/ (2026-06-03)
- Accessed: 2026-07-23
- Relevance to decision class: high

## Extractable claims

1. Search Console Settings → Search generative AI lets property owners Include or Exclude the site from AI Overviews, AI Mode, and generative AI features in Discover (list may expand). (evidence)
2. Exclude prevents links/content from appearing in those features and from being used as input to ground AI responses there; the property receives no traffic or impressions from those features. (evidence)
3. The control is not used as a ranking or inclusion signal for other parts of Search. (evidence)
4. The control does not affect AI model training; use Google-Extended for training limits. Use noindex to leave Google Search entirely. (evidence)
5. After a change, content is generally excluded within 1–2 days once the control is live; some content may take longer due to cache/propagation. (evidence)
6. Rollout is to a subset of website owners during testing; not all properties may see the control yet. (evidence)
7. Google’s June 2026 Keyword blog states AI Overviews has over 2.5B MAU and AI Mode has surpassed 1B monthly users (company claim; useful scale context, not a site-level metric). (evidence)

## Notes

Decide/kill: measuring before opting out matters. Inheritance rules exist for parent/child properties.
