# Google: AI features and your website

- URL: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features
- Accessed: 2026-07-23
- Relevance to decision class: high
- Recency note: Listed in Google updates trail; content current as of access date (post–Nov 2025 guidance set).

## Extractable claims

1. There are no additional technical requirements beyond being indexed and eligible to appear in Google Search with a snippet to be shown as a supporting link in AI Overviews or AI Mode. (evidence)
2. You do not need new machine-readable files, AI text files, or special schema.org structured data to appear in these features. (evidence)
3. AI Overviews and AI Mode may use query fan-out; they may use different models/techniques, so supporting links can differ. (evidence)
4. AI Overviews are only shown when systems determine they are additive to classic Search, and often do not trigger. (evidence)
5. Sites appearing in AI features are included in overall Search Console Web Performance traffic; clicks from pages with AI Overviews have been described by Google as higher quality (more time on site). (evidence)
6. robots.txt Googlebot controls crawling for Search; preview/snippet controls (nosnippet, max-snippet, etc.) limit what is shown; Google-Extended is a separate training/grounding control for other Google AI systems. (evidence)
